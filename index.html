CHLOE GRAHAM‑MAJOR

I’m an electrical engineer who builds analytical tools and models that make complex energy systems understandable. My work spans power electronics, photovoltaic modelling, quantitative analysis and energy‑market behaviour — always with the goal of turning technical complexity into clear, actionable insight. I enjoy solving hard problems, designing systems that work in the real world, and understanding how engineering decisions shape commercial outcomes in power markets.

Capstone Project — PV Performance Modelling

A quantitative modelling framework that predicts real‑world performance for perovskite and organic photovoltaic technologies. I adapted the De Soto five‑parameter model and integrated temperature, irradiance and spectral behaviour to build a tool that improves energy‑yield forecasts and supports power‑market and LCOE analysis.
