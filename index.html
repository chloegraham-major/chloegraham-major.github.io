About Me

Love solving complex problems with simple solutions? Me too. As a final-year Electrical Engineering student, I'm fascinated by how everything connects: energy systems, markets and data. I enjoy building tools that turn messy problems into clear, actionable insights and using quantitative analysis to support better decision-making. I think fast, learn faster, and never stop asking questions. Let me show you what I've been working on.
