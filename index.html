About Me

I am a final-year Electrical Engineering student fascinated by how energy systems, markets and data connect. I build analytical tools and models that turn messy problems into clear, actionable insights. I think fast, learn faster, and never stop asking questions. Here is what I have been working on.

Capstone Project: PV Performance Modelling

A quantitative modelling framework that predicts real-world performance for perovskite and organic photovoltaic technologies. I adapted the De Soto five-parameter model and integrated temperature, irradiance and spectral behaviour to build a tool that improves energy-yield forecasts and supports power-market and LCOE analysis.

CubeSat Electrical Power System

As Electrical Power Systems Lead, I designed analytical tools and hardware systems that ensured reliable power generation, storage and distribution for a quantum communications CubeSat. This included energy-budget modelling, converter design, PCB development and system-level optimisation.
