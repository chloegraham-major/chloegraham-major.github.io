CubeSat Electrical Power System

As Electrical Power Systems Lead, I designed the full power architecture for a quantum‑communications CubeSat — combining analytical modelling, circuit design, PCB development and hardware validation. The EPS ensures reliable power generation, storage and distribution in a constrained, mission‑critical environment, integrating solar input, battery management and regulated power delivery.

System Overview

The EPS was designed to deliver stable, efficient power to all CubeSat subsystems under varying orbital conditions. I developed analytical tools to model energy generation, battery behaviour and subsystem loads, ensuring the design met mission requirements and operated safely across all expected scenarios.

Key system capabilities:

  • Maximum Power Point Control (MPPC) for solar optimisation
  • High‑efficiency synchronous buck conversion
  • Wide‑range buck‑boost regulation (LTC3119)
  • Battery protection, sensing and telemetry
  • Regulated 3.3V logic rail and 12.6V bus rail

This work reflects my broader engineering approach: combining modelling, simulation and hardware design to build systems that work reliably in the real world.

DC‑DC Conversion

The EPS uses two main converters, selected and modelled to ensure efficiency, stability and resilience across orbital operating conditions:

LTC3119 — A wide‑range buck‑boost converter providing regulated 12.6V bus power. It supports MPPC, PGOOD signalling and seamless transitions between solar and battery input.

LT8609A — A synchronous buck converter generating the 3.3V logic rail. It offers low‑noise switching, fast transient response and high efficiency.

I validated converter behaviour using LTSpice simulations, analysing transient response, switching performance and load regulation to ensure mission‑ready operation.

Battery Architecture

The CubeSat uses a multi‑cell Li‑ion battery pack with integrated protection, sensing and conditioning circuitry. I modelled battery behaviour under charge/discharge cycles, orbital lighting conditions and subsystem loads to ensure safe and reliable operation.

The architecture includes:

  • Li‑ion Battery_Cell modules
  • PMOS switching for protection and isolation
  • BAT54WS diodes for reverse‑current protection
  • MCP6004 op‑amp for sensing and signal conditioning

These components ensure accurate telemetry, safe operation and robust protection against electrical faults.

Connectors & Interfaces

The EPS provides regulated outputs and telemetry signals to the onboard computer and payload. I designed connector interfaces to ensure reliable integration with CubeSat avionics and to support modular testing during development.

  • SLW‑116‑01‑T‑D main power connector
  • 3.3V logic rail
  • 12.6V bus rail
  • PGOOD status signalling
  • MPPC control interface

These interfaces support safe operation, clear telemetry and robust subsystem integration.
