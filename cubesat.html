CubeSat Electrical Power System

A complete EPS architecture designed for a quantum‑communications CubeSat. This system integrates battery management, regulated power rails, MPPT control, and high‑efficiency DC‑DC conversion using space‑grade components.

System Overview

The EPS provides stable, efficient power for all CubeSat subsystems. It manages solar input, battery charging, regulated distribution, and fault‑tolerant switching between power sources.

Key features include:

  • Maximum Power Point Control (MPPC)
  • High‑efficiency synchronous buck conversion
  • Wide‑range DC‑DC regulation (LTC3119)
  • Battery protection and monitoring
  • 3.3V and 12.6V regulated rails

DC‑DC Conversion

The EPS uses two main converters:

LTC3119 — A wide‑range buck‑boost converter providing regulated 12.6V bus power. It supports MPPC, PGOOD signalling, and seamless transitions between solar and battery input.

LT8609A — A synchronous buck converter generating the 3.3V logic rail. It offers low‑noise switching, fast transient response, and high efficiency.

These converters ensure stable operation across varying solar input conditions and battery states.

Battery Architecture

The CubeSat uses a multi‑cell Li‑ion battery pack with protection circuitry, current sensing, and voltage monitoring.

The schematic includes:

  • Battery_Cell modules
  • PMOS switching for protection
  • BAT54WS diodes for reverse‑current protection
  • MCP6004 op‑amp for sensing and conditioning

These components ensure safe charging, discharging, and accurate telemetry.

Connectors & Interfaces

The EPS provides regulated outputs and telemetry signals to the OBC and payload.

  • SLW‑116‑01‑T‑D main power connector
  • 3.3V logic rail
  • 12.6V bus rail
  • PGOOD status
  • MPPC control

These interfaces ensure reliable integration with the CubeSat’s avionics.
